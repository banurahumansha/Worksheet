Refund & Cancellation Policy

At MapBuddyAI, we want you to be satisfied with our services. If you are unsatisfied, please review our refund policy below.

Cancellations

You may cancel your subscription at any time before the next billing cycle to avoid future charges. Lifetime plan purchases do not require cancellation.

Refunds

We offer a full refund if requested within 7 days of purchase, provided the service has not been substantially used.

  • Subscription Plans (Monthly/Yearly): Refunds available within 7 days of purchase, subject to usage conditions.
  • Lifetime Plans: Refunds available within 7 days of purchase, subject to usage conditions.

Refund requests made after 7 days from purchase, or after substantial use of the service, will not be eligible.

Usage Clause

Refunds are only granted if the service has not been extensively accessed or consumed. We reserve the right to deny refund requests if substantial use is detected prior to the refund request.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please email mapbuddy.app@gmail.com with your purchase details (transaction ID, date, payment method).

Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after approval.

Exceptions

Refunds will not be provided for misuse of the service or failure to cancel before renewal (except within the 7-day refund window).
